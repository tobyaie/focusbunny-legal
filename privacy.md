# FocusBunny Privacy Policy

**Draft — last updated 24 September 2026.**

FocusBunny is a study timer: you set a focus time and Bunny runs a marathon
while you focus. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is
kept and your rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

**Who we are.** FocusBunny is provided by [OWNER LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us"),
the data controller. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect

| Data | When | Why (lawful basis) |
|---|---|---|
| A random installation identifier and an anonymous account identifier | The first time you start a focus session | To give each installation its one guest start and run your timer (contract) |
| Your device's time zone | When your account is created | To work out your daily free start (contract) |
| Your focus sessions: start and end times, chosen duration and pace, pauses, penalties, whether a phone call paused the timer, and the game result | While you use the timer | To run the timer, keep one timer per account, move a timer between your devices, and show your results (contract) |
| Carrots and pantry items | When you earn, buy or use them | To keep your rewards across your devices (contract) |
| Your Apple or Google sign-in: an account identifier and, if the provider shares them, your name and email (Apple may give a private relay address) | Only if you sign in | To keep your account, allowance and subscription across devices (contract) |
| Subscription status from the App Store: product, renewal and expiry dates, and an Apple transaction identifier (never your payment details) | Only if you subscribe | To give you Premium and keep one subscription per account (contract) |
| Security and service records: sign-in times, request logs and error logs | While you use online features | To keep the service working and prevent misuse (legitimate interests) |

**We do not collect:** phone numbers or the identity of callers, which other
apps you use, your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.
There is no advertising and no cross-app tracking. FocusBunny does not
currently collect product analytics.

**Phone calls and device locking.** To pause the timer during calls, the app
uses iOS's CallKit observer on your device. It learns only that a call
started, connected or ended, never who called or the number. It learns that
the screen locked, not what you did.

## Where it is kept and who helps us

- **Google Firebase** (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Authentication)
  stores and processes your account and timer data. Firestore and Cloud
  Functions run in London (`europe-west2`). Firebase Authentication may
  process sign-in data in the United States.
- **Apple and Google** handle Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in, and
  Apple handles App Store purchases under their own privacy policies.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.** (United States) processes App Store subscription
  records for us.

Transfers outside the UK rely on the UK International Data Transfer
Addendum or the providers' standard contractual clauses. We never sell your
data.

- **Guest data that never signs in:** until [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 24

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account** in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  This deletes your account, carrots, pantry and the timer records on our
  servers, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Cancel any subscription separately
  in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
- **Sign out** at any time. Your records stay with your account.
- Under the UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or port your
  data, or object to or restrict its processing. Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. You
  can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Children

FocusBunny is intended for people aged 16 and over.
