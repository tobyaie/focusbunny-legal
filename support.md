# FocusBunny Support

Email **ttoeynattapon@gmail.com**. We aim to reply within two working
days.

## Common questions

**I can't start a focus session.** Starting needs an internet connection.
Guests get one start per installation; after that, sign in for one free start
a day, or subscribe to FocusBunny Premium.

**My timer is running on my other iPhone.** Open FocusBunny on this iPhone and
tap Start. It offers **Take over here**. Anything done on the other iPhone
since it last synced is not counted.

**Restore my subscription.** Tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen or in
Settings → Premium. If you bought Premium while signed in, sign in to that
account first.

**Cancel my subscription.** iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions →
FocusBunny. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

**Delete my account.** Settings → Delete account (at the bottom, when you are
signed in; end any running focus session first). This does not cancel a
subscription; cancel it in iPhone Settings too.
